# Color Palette Used in IPO Analysis App

## 🌑 Base Colors
- bg-background: Base background color (light/dark depending on theme)
- text-foreground: Main text color
- text-muted-foreground: Subdued text for descriptions and secondary labels




## 🎨 Muted Backgrounds
- bg-muted: Soft gray background for neutral sections
- bg-muted/50: Slightly transparent muted section
- bg-muted/30: Used for cards and section dividers
- bg-background/60: Frosted glass style background for overlays

## 🌞 Light & Dark Themes
- text-background: Light-themed text on dark backgrounds
- text-background/80: Faded version for light-on-dark contrast
- dark:text-green-400: Green text variant in dark mode
- dark:border-green-400: Green border in dark mode

## ✅ Highlights & Accents
- text-primary: Brand’s primary highlight color (used for headings, icons)
- border: Standard element border
- border-t: Top border used for dividers

## 📈 Status & Metric Colors
- text-green-600: Indicates positive metrics or growth (GMP, returns)
- border-green-600: Used in outlines for growth-indicative tags

## Usage Tip:
Use these classes consistently to maintain visual cohesion. Keep success and action states in green tones, and structure content around bg-muted layers with contrasting text-muted-foreground overlays.
