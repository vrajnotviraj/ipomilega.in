Tip-channel hype, casino energy, crypto neon, and paper-and-serif nostalgia.
Josef Albers · Interaction of Color (1963)

Colour is relative

The same colour changes with what sits next to it. So brand green must not sit near gain green; C1b pulls them apart in hue, and the blue options remove the clash entirely.

Johannes Itten · The Art of Color (1961)

Complements and extension

Blue and orange are complements, the strongest pairing on his wheel. His contrast of extension says a strong colour needs only a small area, so marigold is kept to one candle and "today" states.

Eva Heller · Psychology of Color

What people feel

In her survey of about 2,000 people, blue was the one colour with no dominant negative feeling, tied to trust and lasting values. Green read as life and freshness, and also as immaturity.

Alina Wheeler · Designing Brand Identity

Consistency builds trust

Her ideals include clarity, consistency, differentiation and authenticity. One mark, one ink, one accent, repeated in the favicon, nav, cards and share images, so every touchpoint reads as the same source.

Marty Neumeier · The Brand Gap, Zag

Be the only one

A brand should finish the sentence "the only ___ that ___". The only IPO tracker whose mark is a listing-day chart: the candle M and the marigold breakout carry the difference, not the ink colour.
